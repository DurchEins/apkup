# What's New

## November 2026

### November 25, 2026 | 1.47.0 (237) | Feature Update

> [Banner] Accessible Mode & WCAG 2.2 | Accessibility

- **Accessible Mode on First Launch & in Settings** A completely tailored high-contrast interface can now be enabled during initial setup or anytime in Settings. It features a fixed non-overlapping bottom bar, larger typography, disabled animations with haptic feedback preserved, and clear visual separation.
- **High-Contrast Solar Theme (Auto / Light / Dark)** The Accessibility theme can follow sunrise and sunset automatically or stay permanently light or dark. Solar transition times can now be adjusted comfortably in 15-minute steps.
- **Clearer Reading & One-Tap Controls** In Accessible Mode, Apps and Updates use single-column lists with multi-line filter chips, labeled scroll buttons, larger touch targets, and a direct Select button on every update card. Hidden apps, skipped updates, confirmation dialogs, and the in-app changelog are also streamlined without distracting overlays.
- **Cleaner Setup Menu & Pre-Download Hash Hint** Redundant layout and view toggles as well as the live preview were removed from Settings, and Dynamic Color is more compact in standard mode. When a source does not provide a checksum before downloading, tapping the subtle shield icon next to the target version explains that Android still verifies the app signature on install.
- **Home Screen Widget & Backup Limits** Added a home screen widget for available updates, configurable storage and retention limits for automatic rollback backups, update history export, and extra confirmation prompts before batch updates.
- **Website & Accessibility Statement** The project website now includes a nine-language accessibility statement alongside a high-contrast three-way theme switcher.
- **General Improvements** Fixed minor visual issues, refined download security checks, and improved overall stability.

### November 22, 2026 | 1.46.0 (231) | Feature Update

- **Upgraded Google Play Engine** Direct on-device Play Store integration without an external dispenser server for stronger privacy alongside faster, more reliable update checks and downloads.

- **Polished Setup Experience** Refined card framing, cleaner spacing, and better organized options make managing rollback backups and update preferences easier to scan.
- **General Improvements** UI polish, refreshed store metadata, and under-the-hood stability and maintenance updates.
