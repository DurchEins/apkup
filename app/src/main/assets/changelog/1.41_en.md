### November 9, 2026 | 1.41.18-Beta (173) | Feature Update

> [Banner] M3 Expressive Design & New Home | + Split Switches

- **New App Home** Clean separation between user and system apps with counters, instant list and grid view toggling, and alphabetical A–Z sticky sections.
- **M3 Expressive Design** Fresh color scheme with Warm Amber and Slate in Dark Mode and Titanium Monochrome in Light Mode, featuring floating navigation and borderless cards.
- **Native APKMirror Direct Downloads** APKMirror updates download directly inside the app with automatic security challenge resolution and without opening an external browser.

- **Autonomous Shizuku Updates** Discovered updates can be installed completely silently in the background with customizable rules for battery and Wi-Fi.
- **Wi-Fi Download Options** Dedicated bottom sheet for network constraints, periodic background check conditions, and charging-only downloads.
- **Streamlined Advanced Settings** App management with clear navigation for hidden apps and skipped versions, system permissions, and backup tools consolidated into a single collapsible section.
- **Hardware-Backed Encryption** API tokens and app preferences are securely protected using Android Keystore encryption directly on your device.
- **General Improvements** Stutter-free scrolling, smoother animations, refined touch gestures, and comprehensive stability enhancements.
