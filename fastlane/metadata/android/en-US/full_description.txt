ApkUp is a modern, privacy-first open-source Android app updater built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive design. It discovers, manages, and installs updates across multiple sources without ads or tracking.

Key Features:
* Multi-Source Discovery: Check updates across Google Play Store, APKMirror (with direct in-app downloads and .apkm split-bundle extraction), IzzyOnDroid (F-Droid Index V2), GitHub, and GitLab (including self-hosted instances and subgroups).
* Autonomous Shizuku Background Updates & History: Automatically download and silently install updates in the background via Shizuku with Wi-Fi-only and charging-only guards, consolidated summary notifications, and an in-app Update History sheet with direct links to previous versions for easy rollbacks.
* 24-Hour Smart APK Cache: Downloaded packages stay ready locally for 24 hours so cancelled or deferred updates can be installed instantly without re-downloading.
* Obtainium Interoperability & Full Backup: Import and export Obtainium repository mappings directly, or back up your entire ApkUp configuration as a single JSON file.
* Signature Protection & Granular Filters: Pre-install certificate mismatch warnings, per-version skipping, permanently hidden apps, and Wi-Fi data saver mode.
* Modern Android Experience: Supports Android 10.0 (API 29) through Android 17 with Dynamic Color (Material You), a floating navigation dock, built-in self-updater, and 9 languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese).
