Privacy Policy for ApkUp (https://github.com/DurchEins/apkup)

ApkUp is an open-source, client-side Android utility licensed under GNU GPLv3.
- No Personal Data Collected: ApkUp does not collect, track, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or user data.
- No Analytics & No Ads: Zero tracking SDKs, zero telemetry, and zero advertisements.
- Network Usage: Network requests connect strictly from the user's device via direct HTTPS to public developer release feeds (GitHub, GitLab, F-Droid) to check version numbers and download public APK files. No intermediary servers exist.
- Contact: apkup.app@gmail.com
Full Privacy Policy: https://github.com/DurchEins/apkup/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
