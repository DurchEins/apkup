Privacy Policy for ApkUp (https://github.com/DurchEins/apkup)

ApkUp is an open-source, client-side Android utility licensed under GNU GPLv3.
- No Developer Backend or Personal Data Collection: ApkUp operates without any developer servers and never collects names, email addresses, location data, IMEIs, or Advertising IDs.
- No Analytics & No Ads: Zero tracking SDKs, zero telemetry, and zero advertisements.
- Explicit Source Consent & HTTPS Networking: Only after explicit user selection on first launch does ApkUp connect via HTTPS to user-enabled update sources (signed F-Droid / IzzyOnDroid indexes, GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg/Forgejo, APKMirror, or anonymous Google Play compatibility checks via Aurora Dispenser). Diagnostic logs strip tokens and are only saved or shared manually by the user.
- Encrypted Local Storage: Settings and optional tokens are encrypted locally via Google Tink (AES-256-GCM) in Android Keystore with cloud backups disabled.
- Contact & Issue Tracker: https://github.com/DurchEins/apkup/issues
Full Privacy Policy: https://github.com/DurchEins/apkup/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
